Education

I think of education as training for how to think and act — not just a list of modules. Below are the learning environments that shaped my approach: curious, responsible, and practice-oriented.

University of Birmingham

MA Digital Media and Creative Industries · Sept 2024 – Sept 2025

This year strengthened my understanding of creative industries through policy, marketing, and media adaptation — and helped me translate ideas into practical outcomes.

  • Focus: digital media, creative industries, and real-world cultural contexts
  • Training: structured thinking, clear communication, and project development

Also completed: The Birmingham Award (employability skills award).

Sun Yat-sen University (SYSU)

Bachelor of Management in Tourism Management · Sept 2020 – Jun 2024

My undergraduate study built a strong foundation in managing experiences and audiences — from event planning and brand thinking to research design.

  • Focus: experience design, events, branding, and research fundamentals
  • Habit: learning by doing — organising, observing, and improving through feedback

Publication

Organizational Greenwashing and Work Engagement: Evidence From the Hospitality Industry

Business Strategy and the Environment · DOI: 10.1002/bse.70163

This research examines how organisational greenwashing relates to employees’ work engagement within the hospitality industry.

What I gained: working on an academic publication taught me how evidence becomes an argument — and how careful thinking turns into clear, decision-useful writing.
